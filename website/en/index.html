05 / FAQ

Important questions.
Direct answers.

Is private information removed automatically?

PaperPath automatically scans for supported kinds of sensitive information on device, but it never deletes findings without review. You choose each item, inspect its PDF page, select the redaction style, and confirm the final action.

Can redacted content be recovered?

No. The corresponding content cannot be recovered from a permanently redacted copy, so PaperPath presents an explicit confirmation first. The source file you imported remains available for another pass.

Are PDFs uploaded to a server?

Core PDF processing, OCR, and privacy detection run on device. Data enters another service only when you choose a system share destination or enable optional iCloud metadata sync.

What can PaperPath do beyond redaction?

Read, fill forms, sign, annotate, scan, OCR, merge, split, organize pages, compress, password-protect, export to Office/images/text, and prepare Verified Output for delivery.

Does PaperPath require a subscription?

No recurring PaperPath subscription is required. The free download includes one Verified Output experience; a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited Verified Output and batch delivery.
