# PaperPath: Secure PDF Studio App Store Metadata

## Primary English

- App name: `PaperPath: Secure PDF Studio` (28/30 characters)
- Subtitle: `Private PDFs. Verified output.` (30/30 characters)
- Launcher display name: `PaperPath PDF`
- Category: Productivity
- Pricing: Free download with one real Verified Output
- Launch unlock: US$9.99 one-time
- Regular unlock: US$19.99 one-time

The App Store name communicates the PDF category and on-device security while
preserving PaperPath as the brand. The shorter launcher name avoids truncation
under the app icon.

## Positioning

PaperPath privately completes important PDFs, preserves the original, and
reopens the finished copy to check it before sharing.

Primary line:

`Finish important PDFs privately, then verify the copy before you share it.`

Do not position PaperPath as the broadest all-in-one editor. Its category is a
private, verified PDF workflow for iPhone and iPad.

## Search Intent

Keyword direction:

`offline,editor,redact,sign,scanner,ocr,forms,merge,compress,privacy,local`

Do not duplicate `PaperPath` or `PDF` from the app name in the keyword field.

## Claims To Avoid

- All-in-one PDF editor
- The most powerful PDF app
- Better than Acrobat
- Everything stays on device
- Certified or legally verified PDF
