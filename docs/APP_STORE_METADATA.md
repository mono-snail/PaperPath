## English description

Finish important PDFs on your iPhone or iPad. Fill a form, place your signature, review private details, and prepare a copy to share while keeping your original.

SIGN AND FINISH
Read, annotate, fill supported PDF forms, and place a handwritten signature. Reorder pages, combine documents, or create a smaller copy when it helps.

REVIEW PRIVATE DETAILS
Scan for supported kinds of sensitive information on your device. Check each candidate in the PDF, choose what to remove, and create a permanently redacted copy. Automatic detection can miss information, so review the document before sharing.

TURN SCANS INTO SEARCHABLE PDFs
Review captured pages, adjust crop and contrast, and recognize text on device. Inspect uncertain text and correct recognition results before creating a searchable copy.

CHECK THE COPY YOU SHARE
Verified Output creates an image-based copy, reopens its pages, and records file checks in a receipt. See warnings, compare source and output information, and share the checked PDF or a ZIP containing the PDF and receipt.

Verified Output flattens the document: searchable text, links, forms, and editable layers are removed from that copy. File checks do not certify the accuracy of document content, a signer's identity, or legal compliance. A handwritten signature is a visual mark.

CORE PROCESSING ON DEVICE
PDF processing and text recognition run locally. Optional iCloud metadata sync, App Store purchases, and actions you choose in the system share sheet have their own network and storage boundaries. No PaperPath account is required.

Download free with one Verified Output experience. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited Verified Output, including batch delivery. No recurring PaperPath subscription.

## 简体中文描述

在 iPhone 和 iPad 上完成重要 PDF：填写表单、放置签名、复核隐私信息，保留原件，再分享处理后的副本。

填写与签名
阅读、批注、填写受支持的 PDF 表单，放置手写签名。整理页序、合并文件，或在确实能缩小时创建压缩副本。

隐私复核
在设备端扫描支持类型的敏感信息，回到 PDF 核对候选，选择需要永久移除的内容。自动识别可能遗漏信息，分享前仍需复核文档。

扫描与文字识别
检查扫描页，调整裁切和对比度，在本机识别文字。优先核对不确定的文字，修正识别结果，再生成可搜索副本。

检查将要分享的那份文件
Verified Output 创建图像型副本，重新打开各页并记录文件检查回执。查看提醒、核对原件与输出信息，分享已检查的 PDF，或将 PDF 与回执一起打包。

Verified Output 会将文档扁平化：该副本不再保留可搜索文字、链接、表单和可编辑图层。文件检查不保证内容准确、签署人身份或法律合规；手写签名属于视觉标记。

核心处理在本机完成
PDF 处理与文字识别在设备端运行。可选的 iCloud 元数据同步、App Store 购买，以及你选择的系统分享服务各有联网与存储边界。无需 PaperPath 账号。

免费下载，包含一次 Verified Output 体验。一次性内购解锁不限次数的 Verified Output，支持批量交付，无需持续订阅 PaperPath。

应用上线后需要真实用户验证首份文件完成率、交付前放弃原因、再次处理文件的比例及付费意愿；此次代码迭代不能证明市场需求或留存。此条为产品研究建议，没有新增遥测或上传行为。
