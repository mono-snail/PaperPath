# PaperPath PDF — Support / 使用帮助

Publication draft for 1.1.0 (4). Add the publisher's real support contact and publish at a working URL before submission. No contact or public URL has been invented.

## English

**Keep a copy of important documents.** PaperPath works with a private copy of imported PDFs. Its private document library is excluded from automatic device backups, and optional iCloud metadata sync does not back up PDFs. Export documents to Files or another location before deleting the app or changing devices.

**Import a PDF.** Use the import action on Home and select the PDF in Files. If a cloud file cannot be opened, let its provider download it first, then retry. A password-protected PDF may require its password.

**A scan or recognition needs correction.** Review the page image, crop, and recognized text. Automatic OCR and privacy detection can miss or misread information. Check the PDF before using or sharing the result.

**An output looks different.** Review the output before sharing. Image-based and flattened copies remove searchable text, links, form fields, and editable layers. Office conversion may change layout; keep the original when it matters.

**A purchase has not unlocked.** Open the purchase screen in Settings. A purchase awaiting approval stays pending until Apple confirms it. Use the status check for a pending purchase or Restore Purchase for an existing purchase, with the Apple Account used to buy it. If the product is unavailable, retry when the App Store is reachable.

**A file seems missing.** Check Documents, Results, and Trash. A generated result can be separate from its source. Files exported to other apps or storage locations are managed there. Deleting the app removes its private library.

**Ask for help.** Include the app version, device/iOS version, the action that failed, and the visible message. You can generate private diagnostics in Settings and choose whether to share them. Do not send confidential PDFs, passwords, or signatures unless you decide they are necessary and safe to disclose. Generating diagnostics does not send them automatically.

## 简体中文

**先保存重要文档。** PaperPath 处理导入后的私有副本。私有文档库不包含在设备自动备份中，可选的 iCloud 元数据同步也不备份 PDF。删除应用或更换设备前，请导出至“文件”或其他位置。

**导入 PDF。** 在首页选择导入，从“文件”选取 PDF。云端文件打不开时，先等待对应服务下载完成再重试；加密 PDF 可能需要输入密码。

**扫描或识别不准确。** 检查页面图像、裁切和识别文字。OCR 与隐私检测可能遗漏或误识别，使用或分享结果前请核对 PDF。

**输出与原件不同。** 分享前先预览输出。图像型及扁平化副本不保留可搜索文字、链接、表单和可编辑图层。Office 转换可能改变布局，重要文件应保留原件。

**购买尚未解锁。** 从设置进入购买页。等待批准的购买需 Apple 确认后才会生效；可检查待批准状态，或使用原购买 Apple 账户恢复已有购买。商品暂不可用时，在 App Store 可连接后重试。

**找不到文件。** 依次检查文档、结果与废纸篓；生成结果可能与源文件分开显示。导出至其他应用或位置的副本需在对应位置管理。删除应用会移除其私有文档库。

**寻求帮助。** 提供应用版本、设备和 iOS 版本、失败步骤及界面提示。设置中可生成私密诊断，再自行选择是否分享；生成不会自动发送。除非你判断确有必要且适合披露，请勿发送机密 PDF、密码或签名。
