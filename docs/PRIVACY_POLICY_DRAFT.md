# PaperPath PDF — Privacy policy draft / 隐私政策草稿

Prepared 2026-09-26 for version 1.1.0. This text has not been published. Before publication, the publisher must add its legal identity, a working privacy contact, and the effective date. The app's offline “Privacy & Data” screen already describes the handling below.

## English

### Documents and local processing

PaperPath does not require a PaperPath account. PDF processing, scan review, text recognition, and privacy detection run on your device. The app does not send PDF contents, extracted text, or handwritten signatures to a PaperPath processing server.

Imported documents are copied into the app's storage. Editing a working copy does not change the source file you imported. Local storage can include PDFs, recognized text, thumbnails, bookmarks, versions, signatures you choose to save, and output receipts.

PaperPath excludes its private document library from automatic device backups. Optional iCloud metadata sync does not back up PDF files. Before deleting the app or replacing your device, export documents you want to keep to Files or another location. Source files and exported copies are handled according to the storage and backup settings of their locations.

### Optional iCloud metadata

Metadata sync is off by default. If you enable it, PaperPath uses your iCloud account to synchronize reading position, bookmarks, tags, workflow state, file fingerprints, and receipt metadata. PDF files and extracted body text are not included in this sync. Tags and receipt metadata may still contain information you consider private. Apple handles iCloud storage under its own terms.

Turning sync off stops synchronization; it does not itself delete data already in iCloud. Use “Delete Synced Metadata” to remove PaperPath metadata from iCloud.

### Permissions and notifications

Camera access supports document and identity-code scanning. App Lock uses system authentication, including Face ID where available; PaperPath does not receive your biometric template. Due-date notifications are optional and do not include PDF names or contents. You can manage permissions through system Settings.

### Purchases

Apple processes payments and restoration. PaperPath uses verified StoreKit purchase status to enable lifetime Verified Output. PaperPath does not receive your payment card details. The unlock is a non-consumable, one-time purchase without recurring charges.

### Sharing and support

When you choose to export or share, the receiving app or service handles that copy under its own policies. Delivery packages can include document and receipt metadata. Review them before sharing.

If you choose to share private diagnostics, the generated file contains app/device information, document counts, storage totals, and relevant settings. It excludes PDF bytes, file names, extracted text, passwords, and signatures. Generating the file does not automatically send it to the developer.

### Advertising and analytics

The app includes no advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK. Apple may separately provide store or crash information according to your device and App Store choices.

### Retention and deletion

Manage working copies and Trash within the app. “Delete All Local Data” removes local PaperPath documents and related data without deleting the source files you imported. Synced metadata has its own deletion control. Exported copies and files or backups held outside PaperPath must be managed separately.

### Privacy review limits

Automatic privacy detection may miss information. Review each document before sharing. Permanent redaction cannot be undone in its resulting copy. File and page checks do not certify content accuracy, identity, or legal compliance.

### Policy changes

Publish the effective date with each revision. If data handling changes, update this policy and the app's disclosure before that change is released.

## 简体中文

### 文档与本机处理

PaperPath 无需注册账号。PDF 处理、扫描复核、文字识别及隐私检测在设备端完成；应用不向 PaperPath 处理服务器发送 PDF 内容、提取正文或手写签名。

导入会在应用存储中创建工作副本，不修改导入前的原件。本地数据可能包括 PDF、识别文字、缩略图、书签、历史版本、你选择保存的签名及输出回执。

PaperPath 的私有文档库不包含在设备自动备份中，可选的 iCloud 元数据同步也不会备份 PDF 文件。删除应用或更换设备前，请将需要保留的文档导出至“文件”或其他位置。导入前的原件与已导出的副本，按其所在位置的存储及备份设置处理。

### 可选 iCloud 元数据

同步默认关闭。开启后，阅读位置、书签、标签、工作流状态、文件指纹及回执元数据可经你的 iCloud 账户同步，不包括 PDF 文件及提取正文。标签与回执元数据仍可能包含私密信息。iCloud 存储由 Apple 按其条款处理。

关闭同步不等于删除已存的云端数据。可使用“删除已同步元数据”移除 PaperPath 的 iCloud 元数据。

### 权限与提醒

相机用于文档与身份二维码扫描。应用锁使用系统认证及可用的面容 ID；PaperPath 不获取生物识别模板。到期提醒为可选功能，通知中不含 PDF 名称和正文。系统权限可在系统设置中管理。

### 购买

Apple 处理付款与恢复购买。PaperPath 根据 StoreKit 验证后的购买状态解锁可信输出，不获取银行卡信息。终身解锁属于一次性非消耗型内购，不自动续费。

### 分享与支持

主动导出或分享后，目标应用或服务按其规则处理副本。交付包可能包含文档与回执元数据，请在分享前核对。

私密诊断文件包含应用/设备信息、文档数量、存储统计及相关设置，不含 PDF 内容、文件名、提取正文、密码或签名。生成诊断不会自动发送给开发者。

### 广告与分析

应用未集成广告或第三方分析 SDK。Apple 可能依据你的系统与商店设置另行提供商店或崩溃信息。

### 保存与删除

工作副本及废纸篓可在应用内管理。“删除全部本地数据”移除本地 PaperPath 文档及关联数据，保留导入前原件。云端元数据需单独删除；已导出的副本和 PaperPath 以外的文件或备份需分别管理。

### 隐私复核边界

自动隐私检测可能遗漏，分享前应复核文档。输出副本中的永久脱敏无法撤销。文件与页面检查不认证内容准确性、身份或法律合规性。

### 政策更新

公开版本需标明生效日期。数据处理方式变更时，应在相应应用版本发布前同步更新政策及应用内说明。
